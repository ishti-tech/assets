 We were Revora's first institutional investor at pre-seed and doubled down in this round alongside Conjunction Capital, F6 Ventures, Hi2 Global, Orbit Startups, and strategic angels including Salman Butt (co-founder of Salla, Shopify of MENA) and operators from Bolt, Mubadala, and EY
 
 Raise coincides with a rebrand from MyAlice to Revora: the product started as a shared inbox for WhatsApp and Instagram conversations and has evolved into a full AI sales layer that recommends products, recovers carts, and completes payment without a human in the loop
 
 Revenue hitting $2.1M annually, up 10× since refocusing on Saudi Arabia and the GCC in late 2024, now its largest and fastest-growing market
 
 Live in 21+ countries; merchants report 15–20% revenue increases from the platform
 
 Proceeds go toward deepening the GCC push and continued product development

 Launched May 12, backed by 39 Bangladeshi commercial banks
 
 $35M inaugural fund targeting late-seed to Series A startups
 
 I serve as a Consultant to BSIC and laid out the fund's governance model at launch: a local investment team, a global investment committee, an international advisory committee, and a five-member board of bank MDs
 
 Our involvement reflects Anchorless's broader push to bring institutional domestic capital into the Bangladesh startup ecosystem, alongside our own direct investing
